What does "backtrack" mean?	To taxi along the runway in use in the direction opposite to the landing/take-off flow (definition). Phraseology: pilot "request backtrack"; ATC "backtrack approved" or "backtrack runway (number)". (recorrer la pista en sentido contrario)	ATC::EN02AV NV
How does a controller instruct an aircraft to follow another?	"Follow (description of other aircraft or vehicle)" (siga a): e.g. "follow the red and white Antonov". Conditional taxi clearances use livery and colour to avoid ambiguity.	ATC::EN02AV
How does ATC tell a pilot to accept the next frequency?	"Contact (unit) (frequency)": "Contact Northern Control 132.6". Pilot reads back unit and frequency.	ATC::EN02AV
Which phrase starts a distress call?	"MAYDAY, MAYDAY, MAYDAY" (socorro). A distress call = a situation where the aircraft requires immediate assistance.	ATC::EN02AV
Which phrase starts an urgency call?	"PAN PAN, PAN PAN, PAN PAN" (urgencia). An urgency message = situation not requiring immediate assistance.	ATC::EN02AV
What must a distress/urgency message contain (SKYbrary version)?	Name of the station addressed, callsign, nature of emergency, fuel endurance and persons on board; plus any supporting information (position, level, speed, heading, pilot's intentions).	ATC::EN02AV
CAP 413 order of an emergency message (8.13)	1) MAYDAY x3 or PAN PAN x3; 2) station addressed; 3) callsign; 4) type of aircraft; 5) nature of the emergency; 6) intention of the person-in-command; 7) position, level, heading; 8) pilot qualifications; ...	ATC::EN02AV
Which transponder code means emergency (emergencia)?	7700. CAP 413 8.10.	ATC::EN02AV
Which transponder code means radio failure (fallo de comunicaciones)?	7600.	ATC::EN02AV
Which transponder code means unlawful interference / hijack (interferencia ilícita)?	7500. CAP 413 8.10 "Hijack or other act of violence".	ATC::EN02AV
Which frequency is the international VHF emergency frequency?	121.5 MHz. If the initial call cannot be made on the frequency in use, squawk 7700 and call on 121.5. SKYbrary.	ATC::EN02AV
What phrase is used when a pilot must transmit without hearing anyone (radio failure)?	"TRANSMITTING BLIND", followed by the message sent twice; if the receiver failed: "Transmitting blind due to receiver failure". (transmitiendo a ciegas) CAP 413 2.88.	ATC::EN02AV
Are "fuel emergency" and "fuel priority" recognized terms?	No. A crew short of fuel must declare PAN PAN or MAYDAY to be sure of being given the appropriate priority. SKYbrary.	ATC::EN02AV
How does ATC acknowledge a MAYDAY?	"Big Jet 345, Roger MAYDAY." (Recibido el MAYDAY.) Then it gives vectors or instructions.	ATC::EN02AV
Which words does the pilot say to announce that a controller's transmission was blocked?	"Transmission blocked".	ATC::EN02AV
What should a crew do if unsure about a clearance?	Ask for clarification ("Confirm ...", "Say again") and read back all items. Never guess: "If in doubt, check!"	ATC::EN02AV
What is the most common cause of a level bust according to the SKYbrary guide?	A correct readback followed by incorrect action. Strategy: note down the clearance before reading it back.	ATC::EN02AV
Which type of error is the biggest causal factor in level busts and runway incursions in Europe?	Communication error. SKYbrary guide introduction.	ATC::EN02AV
Should a pilot ask for reduced wake vortex separation?	No: controllers do not have discretion to grant it. SKYbrary.	ATC::EN02AV
Meaning of "Roger" in RTF as opposed to English "OK"	"Roger" = I have received your last transmission. It does not mean "yes", nor "I will do it" (that is "Wilco"). (Recibido.)	ATC::EN02AV
Meaning of "Wilco" vs "Roger"	Wilco = I understand and will comply. Roger = I have received. Wilco implies Roger. Neither is enough for readback items.	ATC::EN02AV
When the hold-short instruction is given, what does a vehicle driver read back?	"Holding short" (or "holding") followed by the callsign; not Roger/Wilco.	ATC::EN02AV
Transfer of communications: what must ATC give?	The identity of the unit to be contacted and the frequency to be used, in a single message. CAP 413 2.57-2.58.	ATC::EN02AV
May a pilot change frequency on his own in controlled airspace?	No: aircraft in controlled airspace must obtain permission from the controlling authority before changing frequency. CAP 413 2.60.	ATC::EN02AV
What phrase does a controller use to ask the pilot to report his intentions?	"Request your intentions." (Comunique intenciones.) CAP 413 (several examples).	ATC::EN02AV
Meaning of "Standby" in a phraseology context	Wait; no approval or denial is implied and no onward clearance to be assumed. (Espere.)	ATC::EN02AV
Plain language: when may it be used?	Only when standard phraseology cannot serve the intended transmission (Annex 10 Vol II 5.1.1.1). (lenguaje claro)	ATC::EN02AV
What is the purpose of the word "break break"?	Separates messages to different aircraft on a busy frequency. Caution: it can be confused with an instruction to an aircraft formation.	ATC::EN02AV
What is a SAFETYCOM?	A transmission for safety purposes made only within 10 NM of the aerodrome of intended landing and below 2000 ft above the aerodrome elevation (UK). CAP 413 2.7.	ATC::EN02AV
What is meant by the terms "clearance limit"?	The point to which an aircraft is granted an ATC clearance; for taxi, the point where the aircraft must stop unless further permission is given. (límite de autorización) for taxi sense (CAP 413 4.12).	ATC::EN02AV
What is a "readback" (colación)?	The repetition by the pilot of an ATC clearance or instruction so that the controller can check it was understood. (colación) definition wording; requirements in CAP 413 2.68-2.69.	ATC::EN02AV NV
What is "hearback"?	The controller's listening to the pilot's readback to check it is correct.	ATC::EN02AV NV
